Dashboard

An at-a-glance summary of your financial position: net wealth, annual cashflow surplus or deficit, total tax, and Centrelink age pension entitlement. Shows goal progress bars for retirement, debt elimination, and custom savings goals, plus scenario buttons that instantly model what-if moves — retire two years earlier, add $200/week to super, downsize at 70, or cut expenses by 10%. Compares your current situation ("Now") against an advice scenario ("After Advice") to quantify the dollar value of financial planning decisions.

Goals

Define and track multiple financial goals: retirement readiness, paying off debt, buying a home, a career sabbatical, children's education, travel, or a custom savings target. Each goal shows a progress bar driven by live projection data, and expands to suggest the specific inputs (salary sacrifice, retirement age, expense level) that would move the needle most.

Personal

Core demographic inputs: single or couple, ages, planned retirement age, life expectancy, homeowner status, and macroeconomic assumptions (inflation rate, cash rate). These drive every downstream projection. Supports planning for couples with independent ages and different retirement dates.

Income

Enter salary, salary sacrifice (concessional super contributions), dividends, rental income, and other income sources per person. Models employer SG contributions (11.5% FY 2025–26), voluntary concessional contributions up to the $30,000 annual cap, and the carry-forward rule for unused prior-year cap space.

Assets

Manage superannuation balances (accumulation and account-based pension phase), non-super investments, lifestyle assets, and the family home. Includes downsizer contribution modelling for homeowners aged 55 and over. Tracks investment allocation across return profiles (Conservative, Balanced, Growth, High Growth, or custom asset-class splits).

Expenses

Model lifestyle spending across three retirement phases: go-go (active early retirement), slow-go (reduced activity), and no-go (later life). Supports one-off future expenses such as renovations, travel, or gifting to children, each placed in a specific future year. Expenses are in today's dollars and automatically inflated in the projection engine.

Liabilities

Enter mortgages, personal loans, and other debts with balance, interest rate, and repayment amount. The engine models full amortisation year by year and shows the projected payoff date. Multiple debts are supported with individual payoff tracking in the cashflow waterfall.

Projections

Year-by-year projection table and charts from today to life expectancy. Shows super balance, non-super investments, net assets, total tax, Centrelink age pension, and annual cashflow in today's dollars (real terms, deflated by inflation). Supports the Now vs After Advice scenario overlay so you can see the long-run impact of planning decisions.

Cashflow Ledger

A detailed year-by-year breakdown of every income and expense line item: employment income, super drawdowns, Centrelink age pension, investment income, loan repayments, living expenses, tax, and the net surplus or deficit. Helps identify the years where spending exceeds income and shows how the cashflow waterfall draws on cash buffer, investments, and super.

Stress Testing (Monte Carlo)

Runs hundreds of simulated retirement scenarios with randomised annual returns drawn from a normal distribution to estimate the probability of not exhausting assets before life expectancy. Shows median, 10th percentile, and 90th percentile outcomes to quantify downside retirement risk and sequence-of-returns risk.

Aged Care

Basic modelling of residential aged care costs including the means-tested care fee (MTCF) and daily accommodation payment (DAP or refundable accommodation deposit equivalent). Helps households understand the potential aged care liability and its impact on estate planning.

Legislation (Tax & Super Rules)

Displays and allows editing of the ATO tax brackets, Medicare levy thresholds (including Medicare levy surcharge), superannuation parameters (concessional cap, non-concessional cap, contribution tax, preservation age), and Centrelink rules (assets test thresholds, taper rates, deeming rates, gifting limits). Updated to FY 2025–26 by default; future-year rule sets can be selected from the header.

Returns & Portfolios

Edit expected return assumptions for each asset class (Australian shares, international shares, property, bonds, cash) and create or customise return profiles. Default profiles include Conservative (30% growth), Balanced (60%), Growth (80%), and High Growth (100%). Fees are modelled as a drag on gross returns.

Settings

Configure the cashflow waterfall priority (cash buffer target, debt repayment rules), choose a visual theme (Covenant Wealth, Default, Apple, Dark), and export or import your plan as a JSON file for backup and sharing.

Key Australian planning concepts covered

  • Superannuation preservation age (60) and condition of release
  • Account-based pension (ABP) minimum drawdown rates by age band (SIS Reg 1.06(9A))
  • Transition-to-retirement (TTR) income streams
  • Concessional contributions cap ($30,000 FY 2025–26) and carry-forward unused cap
  • Non-concessional contributions cap ($120,000) and the bring-forward rule
  • Centrelink assets test thresholds and $3 taper rate per $1,000 above threshold
  • Centrelink income test deeming rates on financial assets (0.25% / 2.25% FY 2025–26)
  • Gifting rules: $10,000 per financial year, $30,000 cap over five financial years
  • Downsizer contribution for homeowners aged 55+ (up to $300,000 per person)
  • Medicare levy (2%) and Medicare levy surcharge thresholds
  • Franking credits on Australian share dividends
  • All projections in today's dollars (real terms) — no nominal/real toggle confusion
